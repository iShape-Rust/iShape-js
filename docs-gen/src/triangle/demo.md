# Demo

This section contains interactive examples of **iTriangle** triangulation, tessellation, and Minkowski operations.

- **[Triangulation](./triangulation.md)** – Basic polygon triangulation.
- **[Tessellation](./tessellation.md)** – Subdivision of polygons into smaller.
- **[Minkowski](./minkowski.md)** – Sum and difference of two shapes with selectable presets and draggable vertices.
